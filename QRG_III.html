QRG III — Quantum Microphysics of the Substrate

This page will contain the full HTML version of Quantum Resonance Gravity III. The paper develops the microscopic physics underlying the resonance substrate, including:

  • a quantum‑fluid or correlated‑medium model of the substrate
  • a microscopic Hamiltonian leading to the resonance equation
  • the origin and meaning of the coupling constant $g_R$
  • emergence of Lorentz‑invariant excitations without curvature
