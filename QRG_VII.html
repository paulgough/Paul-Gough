Summary

QRG VII develops the cosmological sector of the resonance framework, deriving the modified Friedmann equations, perturbation dynamics, and large‑scale structure predictions implied by the scalar–tensor system introduced in QRG I–VI. The theory naturally reproduces the background expansion history of ΛCDM while introducing controlled deviations in the growth of structure through the gravitational slip $(\Phi \neq \Psi)$.

The scalar potential, motivated by renormalization‑group flow, drives late‑time cosmic acceleration without requiring a fundamental cosmological constant. Slip‑driven modifications to clustering reduce the required dark‑matter fraction at galactic and cluster scales, providing a unified interpretation of the reduced dark sector.

The paper demonstrates that QRG is a viable infrared extension of general relativity, consistent with current cosmological data while offering new observational signatures for upcoming surveys.

Key Results

  • Modified Friedmann equations derived from the resonance scalar field.
  • ΛCDM background reproduced without a fundamental cosmological constant.
  • Slip‑driven modifications to structure growth.
  • Reduced dark‑matter requirement at galactic and cluster scales.
  • Predictions for deviations in weak lensing and redshift‑space distortions.
  • Cosmological consistency of the full QRG programme (I–VII).
