Galactic Dynamics, Gravitational Slip, and the Reduced Dark Sector

Summary

QRG IV applies the scalar–tensor structure developed in QRG I–III to galactic and cluster‑scale dynamics. The key result is that the gravitational slip $(\Phi \neq \Psi)$—a natural prediction of the resonance framework—produces enhanced lensing and rotational support without requiring a full cold‑dark‑matter halo. This leads to a reduced dark sector interpretation in which part of the phenomenology attributed to dark matter arises from the scalar resonance field.

The paper derives rotation curves, lensing profiles, and dynamical mass estimates within the QRG framework, showing consistency with observed galactic scaling relations. Slip‑driven dynamics also provide a natural explanation for the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation and the radial acceleration relation.

These results form the bridge between the astrophysical predictions of QRG and the cosmological dynamics developed in QRG VII.

Key Results

  • Slip‑driven enhancement of gravitational potentials $(\Phi \neq \Psi)$.
  • Rotation curves reproduced without full CDM halos.
  • Lensing consistent with observed mass–light discrepancies.
  • Reduced dark sector interpretation: part of “dark matter” is resonant scalar structure.
  • Consistency with baryonic Tully–Fisher and radial acceleration relations.
  • Framework for cosmological extension in QRG VII.
