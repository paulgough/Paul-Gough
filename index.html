Quantum Resonance Gravity

Quantum Resonance Gravity (QRG) is a multi‑paper research programme developing a resonance‑based description of gravitational interaction. The framework models gravity as the collective dynamics of an underlying physical substrate, with classical spacetime emerging as the long‑wavelength limit of resonance behaviour.

About the Programme

The QRG series develops a unified resonance‑based description of gravitational phenomena, beginning with the classical scalar limit and extending to tensor modes, quantum microphysics, and cosmology. Each paper builds on the previous one, forming a coherent theoretical arc.
