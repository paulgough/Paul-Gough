Quantum Resonant Gravity

Quantum Resonant Gravity (QRG) is an infrared scalar–tensor framework in which a Goldstone-like scalar field and its normalized gradient define a dynamical preferred direction. Matter and light couple to distinct emergent metrics, and gravitational phenomena arise from resonance dynamics in an underlying physical substrate.

The QRG research programme is presented across a sequence of papers, each extending the framework into new physical domains:

  • QRG I: Foundations of the Infrared Scalar–Tensor Framework
  • QRG II: Tensor Modes, Traceless Sourcing, and Wave Phenomenology
  • QRG III: Quantum Microphysics, Substrate Hamiltonian, and Excitations
  • QRG IV: Galactic Dynamics, Gravitational Slip, and the Reduced Dark Sector
  • QRG V: Metric Coupling, Light Propagation, and Observational Signatures
  • QRG VI: Stability, Causality, and the Infrared Limit
  • QRG VII: Cosmology and the Dark Sector — Matching and Extending ΛCDM
