Quantum Resonance Gravity

Quantum Resonance Gravity (QRG) is a resonance-based framework in which gravitational phenomena arise from the collective dynamics of an underlying physical substrate. Instead of treating gravity as geometric curvature, QRG interprets gravitational behaviour as variations in the local resonance amplitude of a universal medium.

The QRG research programme is presented across four papers, each extending the framework into new physical domains:

  • QRG I: Weak-field scalar sector and classical correspondence
  • QRG II: Full sourcing structure and tensor resonance modes
  • QRG III: Quantum microphysics of the substrate
  • QRG IV: Cosmology and large-scale resonant dynamics
