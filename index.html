Quantum Resonance Gravity

Quantum Resonance Gravity (QRG) is a resonance-based framework in which gravitational phenomena arise from the collective dynamics of an underlying physical substrate. Instead of treating gravity as geometric curvature, QRG interprets gravitational behaviour as variations in the local resonance amplitude of a universal medium.

The QRG research programme is presented across four papers, each extending the framework into new physical domains:

  • QRG I: A Scalar–Tensor Effective Field Theory with a Dynamical Preferred Direction
  • QRG II: Nonlinear Kinetic Dynamics and Low-Acceleration Phenomenology
  • QRG III: Cosmology, Background Evolution, and the Low-Acceleration Scale
  • QRG IV: Microscopic Origins and Emergent Infrared Geometry
  • QRG V: Emergent Nonlinear Scalar Dynamics and the Origin of the Low-Acceleration Regime
  • QRG VI: The General-Relativistic Limit and Precision Tests of Gravity
  • QRG VII: Cosmology and the Dark Sector: Matching and Extending ΛCDM
