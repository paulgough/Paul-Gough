Quantum Resonant Gravity — Overview

Quantum Resonant Gravity (QRG) is an infrared scalar–tensor framework in which a Goldstone-like scalar field and its normalized gradient define a dynamical preferred direction. Matter and light couple to distinct emergent metrics, and gravitational phenomena arise from resonance dynamics in an underlying physical substrate.
