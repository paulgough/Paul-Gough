Quantum Resonance Gravity — Overview

Quantum Resonance Gravity (QRG) is a resonance-based framework in which gravitational phenomena arise from the collective dynamics of an underlying physical substrate. Classical spacetime emerges as the long-wavelength limit of this resonance behaviour.
