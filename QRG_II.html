Quantum Resonant Gravity II:
Tensor Modes, Traceless Sourcing, and Wave Phenomenology

Paul Gough
Independent Researcher
Email: paulgough@live.com
Report number: QRG‑II/2026

PDF version: Download QRG II (PDF)

Summary

QRG II extends the scalar–tensor structure introduced in QRG I by developing the full tensor sector of the theory. The scalar field’s normalized gradient defines a preferred direction, and this structure naturally leads to a decomposition of the stress–energy tensor into longitudinal and traceless components. QRG II shows that the traceless sector sources transverse resonance waves—tensor modes analogous to gravitational waves.

These tensor modes propagate on the optical metric rather than the matter metric, leading to a clean separation between gravitational and electromagnetic sectors. The resulting wave phenomenology reproduces the weak‑field predictions of general relativity while introducing subtle, testable deviations in propagation speed, polarization structure, and coupling hierarchy.

This paper establishes the mathematical foundation for the quantum microphysics explored in QRG III and the astrophysical and cosmological applications developed in QRG IV–VII.

Key Results

  • Derivation of the traceless sourcing structure for tensor modes.
  • Identification of transverse resonance waves as the QRG analogue of gravitational waves.
  • Propagation on the optical metric, distinct from the matter metric.
  • Weak‑field correspondence with GR, including wave speed and polarization.
  • Predictions for small deviations in wave propagation and coupling.
  • Framework for the quantum and cosmological developments in later papers.
