About

Paul Gough is an independent theoretical physicist developing the Quantum Resonance Gravity (QRG) framework — a resonance‑based approach to gravity, electromagnetism, and the emergence of particle structure.

His research work focuses on building a coherent physical foundation in which gravitational behaviour arises from variations in a universal resonance field, and where particle‑like excitations emerge naturally from the quantum structure of that field. The Foundational QRG Papers establishes the classical, electromagnetic, and quantum layers of this framework.

Paul’s background spans mechanical and electrical engineering, with extensive experience in applied physics, system design, and technical problem‑solving. His current research programme aims to develop a unified resonance‑field model with predictive structure and empirical testability.
