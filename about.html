About

Paul Gough is an independent theoretical physicist developing the Quantum Resonance Gravity (QRG) framework — a resonance‑based approach that provides a physical mechanism for gravitational behaviour and unifies it with electromagnetic sourcing and the emergence of particle structure.

QRG proposes that gravity arises from variations in the resonance frequency of a universal field sourced by matter and electromagnetic energy. This mechanism offers a coherent explanation for gravitational time dilation, classical gravitational effects, and the connection between energy density and curvature‑like behaviour. The framework extends naturally into quantum structure, where particle‑like excitations emerge as stable resonance modes.

Paul’s background spans mechanical, electrical, and civil engineering, with extensive experience in applied physics, system design, and technical problem‑solving. His current research programme focuses on establishing a unified resonance‑field foundation that addresses conceptual gaps in modern gravitational theory and provides a testable, physically grounded alternative to purely geometric interpretations.
