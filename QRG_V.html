Summary

QRG V develops the observational sector of the theory by analysing how matter and electromagnetic fields couple to the two emergent metrics defined by the scalar resonance field. The distinction between the matter metric and the optical metric leads to modified predictions for lensing, redshift, time delay, and photon propagation, while remaining consistent with existing precision tests.

The paper derives the full set of observational signatures associated with the resonance framework, including the behaviour of null geodesics, the Shapiro delay, gravitational redshift, and lensing profiles. These effects arise naturally from the scalar–tensor structure established in QRG I–II and the microphysical interpretation developed in QRG III.

The results of QRG V provide the empirical bridge between the astrophysical predictions of QRG IV and the cosmological dynamics explored in QRG VII.

Key Results

  • Derivation of the matter and optical metrics from the scalar resonance field.
  • Modified null geodesics and light propagation.
  • Predictions for lensing, redshift, and Shapiro delay.
  • Consistency with precision tests of GR in the solar system.
  • Identification of observational signatures unique to QRG.
  • Framework linking astrophysical and cosmological predictions.
